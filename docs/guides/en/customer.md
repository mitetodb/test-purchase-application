# Customer User Guide

Welcome! This guide is for **Customers** who create and track their own test purchases.

## Your Role

As a **Customer**, you:

✅ **Can do:**
- View and create your own test purchases
- Add items to your purchases
- Upload attachments to your purchases
- View status and history of your purchases
- View shops
- Edit your own profile

❌ **Cannot do:**
- See other customers' purchases
- Assign mystery shoppers
- **See mystery shopper names** (confidential)
- Change purchase status (except DRAFT → SUBMITTED)
- Edit purchases after assignment
- View all customers or users
- Create or edit shops

**Important:** Mystery shopper names are confidential. You'll see "Mystery Shopper" or "Assigned" instead of the actual name.

## Creating a Test Purchase

### Step 1: Start a New Purchase

1. Click **Test Purchases** → **Create**
2. Fill in basic information:
   - **Shop**: Select the location to be evaluated
   - **Description**: What should be evaluated? Be specific!
   - **Due Date**: When do you need results?
3. Click **Create**

The purchase is created in **DRAFT** status.

### Step 2: Add Items

Items are products or services to be evaluated.

1. Open your new purchase
2. Click **Add Item**
3. Fill in:
   - **Item Name**: Product or service name
   - **Quantity**: How many?
   - **Price per Unit**: Expected price
4. Click **Save**
5. Repeat for all items

**Pricing is calculated automatically** by the system:
- Total item cost
- Service fee
- Postage (if applicable)
- Grand total

### Step 3: Submit for Assignment

When your purchase is ready:
1. Review all details
2. Click **Change Status**
3. Select **SUBMITTED**
4. Click **Save**

Your purchase is now queued for mystery shopper assignment by your Account Manager.

## Test Purchase Workflow

```
DRAFT → SUBMITTED → ASSIGNED → IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED → REVIEWED
```

**Your actions:**
- **DRAFT**: You're creating and editing
- **SUBMITTED**: Ready for assignment (Account Manager will assign a shopper)

**What happens next:**
- **ASSIGNED**: Shopper assigned (you can't see who)
- **IN_PROGRESS**: Shopper is working on it
- **COMPLETED**: Shopper has finished
- **REVIEWED**: Account Manager has reviewed results

You'll receive email notifications (if enabled) for status changes.

## Viewing Your Purchases

1. Click **Test Purchases**
2. See only your purchases
3. Click any purchase to see:
   - Shop and description
   - Items and pricing
   - Current status
   - Status history
   - Attachments (receipts, photos, reports from shopper)
   - **Mystery Shopper**: Shows "Assigned" (name confidential)

## Editing a Purchase

You can edit purchases **only in DRAFT status** before submission:
1. Open the purchase
2. Click **Edit**
3. Update information
4. Click **Save**

**After submission, you cannot edit.** Contact your Account Manager if changes are needed.

## Adding Items

While in DRAFT:
1. Open the purchase
2. Click **Add Item**
3. Fill in item details
4. Click **Save**

**To edit an item:**
1. Find the item in the list
2. Click **Edit**
3. Update and save

**To delete an item:**
1. Find the item
2. Click **Delete**
3. Confirm

## Uploading Attachments

You can upload reference materials:

1. Open your purchase
2. Scroll to **Attachments**
3. Click **Upload Attachment**
4. Select file (PDF or image: JPG, PNG)
5. Add description
6. Click **Upload**

**Use cases:**
- Product specifications
- Store layout plans
- Reference photos
- Instructions for shopper

## Viewing Shop Locations

1. Click **Shops**
2. See all available shops
3. Search by name, city, or country
4. View shop details (address, notes)

You cannot create or edit shops. Contact your Account Manager if a shop is missing.

## Status History

Track your purchase progress:
1. Open a purchase
2. Scroll to **Status History**
3. See:
   - Status changes
   - When they occurred
   - Who made the change
   - Comments added

## Email Notifications

You'll receive emails for:
- Purchase assigned to shopper
- Shopper starts work (IN_PROGRESS)
- Shopper completes work (COMPLETED)
- Purchase reviewed (REVIEWED)

Check your email settings in your profile.

## Viewing Results

Once a purchase is **COMPLETED**:
1. Open the purchase
2. View **Attachments** from the shopper:
   - Receipt photos
   - Store photos
   - Evaluation reports
3. Check **Comments** in status history
4. Review pricing breakdown

Your Account Manager will also review and may add comments.

## Understanding Pricing

Prices are calculated automatically:

**Item Costs:**
- Quantity × Price per Unit
- Subtotal for all items

**Service Fee:**
- Calculated by the pricing microservice
- Based on total purchase value

**Postage:**
- Calculated by the pricing microservice
- Based on delivery requirements

**Grand Total:**
- Items + Service Fee + Postage

The pricing updates automatically when you add or change items.

## Best Practices

### Creating Purchases

1. **Be Specific**
   - Describe exactly what to evaluate
   - Provide clear instructions
   - Include any special requirements

2. **Realistic Due Dates**
   - Allow time for assignment and execution
   - Consider shop hours and shopper availability

3. **Complete Item List**
   - Add all items before submitting
   - Include accurate pricing estimates
   - Verify quantities

### Communication

1. **Use Comments**
   - Add notes when submitting
   - Provide context for Account Manager

2. **Upload References**
   - Attach any helpful materials
   - Include product specs or examples

3. **Check Email**
   - Monitor status notifications
   - Respond to questions promptly

### Quality

1. **Review Results**
   - Check all attachments from shopper
   - Verify all items were evaluated
   - Contact Account Manager with questions

2. **Provide Feedback**
   - Share insights with your Account Manager
   - Request clarifications if needed

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Edit Purchase

- Check status: Only DRAFT can be edited
- After submission, contact Account Manager

### Can't See a Purchase

- You can only see your own purchases
- Check filters (status, date)
- Contact Account Manager if missing

### Pricing Looks Wrong

- Verify item quantities and prices
- Pricing is calculated automatically
- Contact Account Manager to verify

### Missing Shop

- You can only select from existing shops
- Contact Account Manager to add a shop

### Upload Fails

- Check file type (PDF, JPG, PNG only)
- File may be too large
- Contact Admin if persistent
