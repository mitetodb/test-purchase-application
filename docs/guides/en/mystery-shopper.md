# Mystery Shopper User Guide

Welcome! This guide is for **Mystery Shoppers** who execute test purchase assignments.

## Your Role

As a **Mystery Shopper**, you:

✅ **Can do:**
- View test purchases assigned to you
- Update status of your assignments
- Upload attachments (photos, receipts, reports)
- Add comments to status changes
- View shops
- Edit your own profile

❌ **Cannot do:**
- See other shoppers' assignments
- Create test purchases
- Assign yourself to purchases
- View all customers
- Create or edit shops or customers
- See purchases not assigned to you

**Important:** Your name is confidential. Customers and Sales Managers cannot see your identity - only Admins and Account Managers can.

## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Go to https://testkauf.getinweb.de
2. Enter your username and password
3. Click **Login**

### Your Profile

1. Click your username → **Profile**
2. Update:
   - Email
   - Phone (important for assignment communication)
   - Country
   - Language
   - Avatar
3. Change password if needed
4. Click **Save**

### Changing Language

Click the globe icon and select:
- **English** (en)
- **Bulgarian** (bg)
- **German** (de)

## Dashboard

Your dashboard shows:
- **Assigned to You**: Purchases awaiting your action
- **In Progress**: Purchases you're currently working on
- **Completed**: Your finished assignments
- **Upcoming Due Dates**: Urgent assignments

## Viewing Your Assignments

1. Click **Test Purchases**
2. See only purchases assigned to you
3. Click any purchase to view:
   - Customer (company name)
   - Shop location and details
   - Description and requirements
   - Items to evaluate
   - Due date
   - Attachments from customer
   - Status and history

**You cannot see:**
- Purchases assigned to other shoppers
- Unassigned purchases
- Customer contact details (contact via Account Manager)

## Assignment Workflow

When assigned to a purchase, it moves through these statuses:

```
ASSIGNED → IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED
```

### Step 1: ASSIGNED

You receive a new assignment:
1. Check email notification (if enabled)
2. Log in and view the purchase
3. Review:
   - Shop location
   - Items to evaluate
   - Due date
   - Special instructions
   - Attached materials
4. Accept the assignment by changing status to **IN_PROGRESS**

### Step 2: IN_PROGRESS

You're working on the assignment:
1. Visit the shop
2. Evaluate the specified items/services
3. Take photos (if required)
4. Collect receipts
5. Take notes for your report

**While working:**
- Upload photos as you go
- Add comments if issues arise
- Contact your Account Manager with questions (via comments or email)

### Step 3: COMPLETED

You've finished:
1. Upload all required files:
   - Receipt photos
   - Store photos
   - Product/service photos
   - Written report (PDF)
2. Change status to **COMPLETED**
3. Add final comments summarizing your findings
4. Enable email notification to alert Account Manager
5. Click **Save**

The Account Manager will review your work.

## Changing Status

### Start Working (ASSIGNED → IN_PROGRESS)

1. Open your assigned purchase
2. Click **Change Status**
3. Select **IN_PROGRESS**
4. Add comment: "Starting work on this assignment"
5. Click **Save**

### Finish Work (IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED)

1. Verify all files uploaded
2. Click **Change Status**
3. Select **COMPLETED**
4. Add comment: Summary of findings
5. Enable **Email Notification**
6. Click **Save**

## Uploading Attachments

This is critical for completing your work.

### Step-by-Step Upload

1. Open your assignment
2. Scroll to **Attachments** section
3. Click **Upload Attachment**
4. Select file:
   - **Receipts**: Photos of purchase receipts
   - **Store Photos**: Exterior, interior, displays
   - **Product Photos**: Items evaluated
   - **Reports**: PDF evaluation report
5. Add description: "Receipt", "Store exterior", "Product X", etc.
6. Click **Upload**

### File Requirements

- **Formats**: JPG, PNG, PDF
- **Size**: Keep images reasonable (under 5 MB each)
- **Quality**: Ensure photos are clear and readable
- **Organization**: Name files descriptively

### What to Upload

**Always upload:**
- Receipt from purchase (clear photo showing items and prices)
- Store exterior photo (for location verification)
- Photos of evaluated items/services

**If requested:**
- Store interior photos
- Employee interaction photos (if allowed)
- Additional documentation
- Evaluation report (typed summary)

## Writing Your Report

Many assignments require a written report.

### Report Template

Create a PDF document including:

**1. Summary**
- Date and time of visit
- Shop location
- Overall impression

**2. Items Evaluated**
For each item:
- Item name and price
- Availability
- Quality assessment
- Staff knowledge
- Any issues

**3. Service Evaluation**
- Staff friendliness
- Response time
- Helpfulness
- Professionalism

**4. Store Environment**
- Cleanliness
- Organization
- Atmosphere
- Accessibility

**5. Recommendations**
- What went well
- Areas for improvement
- Notable observations

**6. Photos Reference**
- List of attached photos and what they show

### Writing Tips

- Be objective and factual
- Include specific details (times, names if appropriate)
- Note positive and negative aspects
- Avoid personal opinions unless requested
- Proofread before uploading

1. Open your assignment

3. See:

## Best Practices

### Preparation

1. **Review Assignment Carefully**
   - Read all instructions
   - Note special requirements
   - Check due date
   - Review attached materials

2. **Plan Your Visit**
   - Verify shop hours
   - Plan transportation
   - Allocate enough time
   - Charge your phone/camera

3. **Gather Materials**
   - Camera/phone for photos
   - Notepad for notes
   - Payment method for purchase
   - Assignment details (printed or on phone)

### During Visit

1. **Be Discreet**
   - Act like a normal customer
   - Don't be obvious about taking notes/photos
   - Follow shop photo policies

2. **Be Thorough**
   - Evaluate all specified items
   - Note relevant details
   - Take clear photos
   - Keep receipts

3. **Be Professional**
   - Treat staff respectfully
   - Don't reveal you're a mystery shopper
   - Follow all shop policies

### After Visit

1. **Upload Promptly**
   - Upload files while visit is fresh
   - Don't wait until due date
   - Organize files clearly

2. **Complete Report**
   - Write while details are fresh
   - Include all required sections
   - Proofread carefully

3. **Change Status**
   - Mark as COMPLETED when done
   - Add summary comment
   - Enable notification

## Communication

### With Account Manager

- Use comments when changing status
- Ask questions early if unclear
- Report problems immediately
- Be responsive to feedback

### Response Time

- Check assignments within 24 hours
- Start work promptly after accepting
- Complete before due date
- Respond to questions quickly

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Access Shop

- Check shop hours
- Verify address
- Contact Account Manager if closed/not found
- Add comment explaining situation

### Can't Find Item

- Check if item is available
- Ask staff for help (as normal customer would)
- Note unavailability in report
- Take photo of alternative if appropriate

### Upload Fails

- Check file size (reduce if needed)
- Verify file type (JPG, PNG, PDF only)
- Try again or contact Admin
- Keep original files as backup

### Unclear Instructions

- Review assignment description
- Check attached materials
- Add comment asking for clarification
- Contact Account Manager

### Due Date at Risk

- Notify Account Manager immediately (via comment)
- Explain situation
- Propose new completion date
- Complete as soon as possible

## Payment and Expenses

**Reimbursement:**
- Keep all receipts
- Upload clear photos of receipts
- Note all expenses in report
- Follow your agreement with Account Manager

**Process:**
- Payment details handled by Account Manager
- Upload receipts for verification
- Follow company reimbursement policy

- ✅ Well-organized
- ✅ Proofread

### Timeliness

- ✅ Accepted quickly
- ✅ Completed before due date
- ✅ Prompt responses
- ❌ Late completions
- ❌ Delayed communication
